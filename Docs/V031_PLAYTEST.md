# v0.3.1 playtest

Copy `Source` and `Config` into your project folder (replace), run `BUILD_EDITOR.bat`, then `OPEN_PROJECT.bat`. The HUD should say **v0.3.1**.

1. **Pumping on the quarters:** ride between the two big quarter-pipes facing each other. Hold **W** the whole time, especially on the way down each ramp. Each air should stay as high or get higher. Let go of W and the airs should slowly shrink.
2. **Manual:** ride fast, hold **Shift**, let go of **W**, then tap W / S to keep the marker in the green.
3. **Flips:** in the air use **G** (backflip) / **F** (frontflip), **Q / E** to spin. W/S/A/D no longer flip you.
4. **Dirt line:** press **2**. Hit the jumps at different speeds. Short or long, it should land or roll over. No more mid-air hangs.
