# v0.4.0 playtest — grinds

Copy `Source` and `Config` into your project folder (replace), run `BUILD_EDITOR.bat`, then `OPEN_PROJECT.bat`. The HUD should say **v0.4.0**.

The rails are on the north side of the park (the silver bars), with the blue ledge near the left one.

1. **Double peg:** ride alongside a rail, a little to one side and heading the same way. Hop (Space) and drift onto it. You should lock on with "DOUBLE PEG GRIND" and a balance meter.
2. **Balance:** keep the gold marker in the green with A / D.
3. **Hop off:** press Space mid-grind and do a trick (e.g. B barspin) before landing.
4. **Grind types:** hold A, D, S or W as you land on the rail for feeble, smith, icepick or toothpick.
5. **Ledge:** grind the long edges of the blue ledge.
6. **Combo chain:** manual → hop → grind → hop off → trick → land holding Shift → manual.

## Please report

- Locking on: too hard or too easy (`GrindSnapDistance` under BMX|Grind)?
- Balance too hard or too easy (`GrindInstability`, `GrindControl`)?
- Does the bike sit at the right height on the rail (`GrindPegHeight`)?
- A recording is ideal.
